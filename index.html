Portfolio

Overview

Hello, my name is Callum, and this is my portfolio, firstly I'd like to welcome you and hope that you like what you see here. I am just an average computer programmer studying computer science at university, however in my spare time I also like to work on personal projects. All my best projects sooner or later will end up here, so please feel free to browse.

This is a portfolio containing all the projects that I have either completed or am currently working on, but it is a collection of projects that I am proud of and wish to share with everyone. Some may not be the nicest or flashiest, but they have been the building blocks of my learning. THis is simply a portfollio of things by me for you and I hope other people can get some use out of the things I have made.

About

As I said previously I am a computer science student currently studying at university, I have experience mainly in C# and know the basics of C++, JavaScript, CSS and HTML. I have about 4 years of experiance with C# and about 3 to 4 years of experiance with Javascript HTML and CSS, however as you can probably see, I much prefer working in C#. I am keen on the idea of open source and keeping things open and non-profit, generally what I make will always be based around that idea.

I much prefer to make software rather than games, however I do have a love for olden style text based RPG's. The general process of me starting a project is "this could be so much easier", so you will probably see that most of my projects are based out that concept too, making life easier.
